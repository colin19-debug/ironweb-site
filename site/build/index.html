/ The details

Payment, timeline and edits.

Payment

50% deposit to start, and the remaining 50% before launch.

Timeline

A standard build takes about one week. The clock starts once the deposit and the materials we need have arrived. Feedback delays move the timeline, and projects with custom integrations are scheduled individually.

Monthly edits

Managed sites include up to one hour a month of edits to existing text, photos, forms and offers. New pages, redesigns and new functionality are quoted separately.

Hosting and ownership

Managed hosting is the default on the IronWeb Build. Once your build is fully paid, you can move on and receive your custom site files and domain information where applicable, or ask for a handoff instead of ongoing management. Automations and funnels that run on the business platform depend on it and don't export as a standalone site.

/ FAQ

Build questions.

Which build is right for me?

If you need a simple site with a few pages and would rather handle hosting yourself, the Compact Build fits. If you want copywriting, more pages, managed hosting and someone to make changes each month, choose the IronWeb Build.

Is the $600 Compact Build really one-time?

Yes. There is no compulsory IronWeb monthly plan. You arrange hosting yourself or add optional managed hosting, and the quote states which.

What happens if I need more than an hour of edits in a month?

Edits beyond the monthly hour, along with new pages, redesigns and new functionality, are quoted before any work starts.

Can I leave and take my site with me?

Once your build is fully paid, yes. You receive your custom site files and domain information where applicable. Platform-dependent automations and funnels stay on the platform.

Do you write the copy?

Professional copywriting is included in the IronWeb Build. For a Compact Build, we work from the content you provide.

What is the free homepage preview?

A personalized first-stage homepage for your business, shared through a private link. It shows the direction, layout and message so you can see what your new site could look like before deciding to move forward. There is no payment or commitment to request one.

What happens after I get in touch?

Colin follows up personally, by email or phone, to learn about your business and what you need. If it is a fit, he creates your homepage preview and shares the link. If you want to go ahead, you get a clear quote.

Do I need to book a call first?

No. You can send a few details without scheduling anything. If you would rather talk it through, you can book a call, but it is optional.

Is the interactive demo on the homepage a real business?

No. The businesses in the demo are illustrative examples, and the demo inquiry is simulated in your browser. For real IronWeb websites, see the Work page.
