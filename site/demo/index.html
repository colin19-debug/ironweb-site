Here's what the business owner sees.

Every inquiry becomes a card on a simple pipeline. As the job moves forward, the card moves with it.

  1. New Lead

    The inquiry lands here the moment it is submitted.

    Demo customerEstimate requestSimulated

  2. Contacted

    Someone has reached out by text or phone.

  3. Estimate Booked

    A visit or call is on the calendar, with reminders.

  4. Quote Sent

    The business sends the estimate. Always a manual step.

    Manual step

  5. Job Scheduled

    The customer said yes and the work has a date.

  6. Job Completed

    Work is done. Review and referral requests go out.

Swipe to see all six stages →

Only the New Lead card reflects your demo request. The other stages are illustrative: the demo never moves a real opportunity forward, and Quote Sent only happens when a business actually sends an estimate.

One system, from first inquiry to finished job.

  • Instant inquiry acknowledgment

    Every form request gets a reply right away, so the customer knows they were heard.

  • Missed-call recovery

    Missed a call on a job site? The caller gets a text back so the conversation keeps going.

  • AI receptionist

    Answers when you cannot, gathers the project details and captures the callback.

  • Estimate scheduling and reminders

    Estimate visits get booked and confirmed, with reminders so nobody forgets.

  • Quote follow-up

    Once you send a quote, polite follow-ups keep it from going quiet.

  • Review and referral requests

    When the job is done, the customer is asked for a review and a referral.
