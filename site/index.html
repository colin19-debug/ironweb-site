/ Straight answers

The usual questions.

What does the free audit cost?

Nothing. You get a report on your Google profile, listings, reviews, website and local map ranking, and Colin walks you through what to fix first. No obligation.

I don't have time to learn new software.

You don't have to learn much. Colin sets everything up preloaded for contractors and walks you through it. Most of it runs without you touching it.

What if it texts the wrong thing?

It won't say anything you haven't approved. You sign off on every message before it goes live, and you can change it any time.

I'm too small for this.

That's who it's for. If you're the one answering the phone, doing the work and sending the invoices, this is the help you don't have time to hire.

What happens if I want to leave?

You take everything with you: your domain, your phone number and an export of your contacts. It was always yours. No contracts that hold you hostage.
