/ FAQ

Automation questions.

Do I need an IronWeb website to use automations?

No. Automations are a separate service. They work alongside your existing site, and we'll confirm how your forms and phone number connect before anything is set up.

What does "Scoped on request" mean?

It's something we can set up for your business, but it isn't a standard, fixed item yet. We'll look at how you work today and quote it before anything is built.

What does usage cost?

It depends on how many messages, calls, emails and AI actions your account uses. Usage is billed separately, and we'll talk through expected volume before you start.

Will texts go out without permission?

Text follow-up only goes to people who have given permission, and anyone can reply STOP to opt out.

Can I start with one and add more later?

Yes. That's the point. Start with the automation that solves your biggest problem, then add more or move to a bundle as your business grows.
