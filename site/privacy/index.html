Who runs this site

This website is operated by Ironweb Digital Growth, doing business as IronWeb Digital, Swansea, Massachusetts. Contact: colin@ironwebdigital.com, (774) 486-7776.

What we collect

When you submit a form, we collect what you enter: your name, email address and phone number, and details about your business such as its name, your trade, your town or service area, your current website and your message. We also record the page you submitted from, how you arrived (for example a referring site or campaign tags in the link), and the time of submission. If you book a call, the booking system collects the details you enter there.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, run the audit or prepare the homepage preview or quote you asked for, schedule calls, and follow up about our services. Submissions are sent securely to our customer management system, which we use to keep track of inquiries and send follow-up messages.

Phone calls

Calls to our number may be answered by an AI receptionist, which takes down your name, number and what you need so we can follow up.

Text messages

If you tick the optional text message box, you agree to receive marketing and informational text messages from Ironweb Digital Growth (IronWeb Digital) about your inquiry and scheduling. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. We record the consent wording you agreed to and when.

We don't sell or share your mobile number or text message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Cookies and browser storage

This site doesn't use advertising cookies. It keeps campaign tags from the link you arrived on (such as utm_source) in your browser's session storage so they can be included with a form submission; this clears when you close the tab. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when serving them. The booking calendar is provided by our customer management platform, which may set its own cookies.

Service providers

We use a website hosting provider, a customer management and messaging platform, and Google Fonts to operate this site and respond to you. They process information on our behalf to provide those services.

How we protect your information

The site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions travel over an encrypted connection to a server function and then to our customer management system, where access is limited to accounts we control and protected by login. Credentials for these systems are never placed in the website's code. We keep inquiry information only as long as it's useful for responding to you and for our business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing colin@ironwebdigital.com. Reply STOP to any text to stop text messages.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
